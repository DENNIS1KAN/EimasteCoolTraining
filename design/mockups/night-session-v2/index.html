What changed, and why.
  • Light theme keeps the brand. v1 documents that volt on white is 1.1:1, so every accent text token fell back to ink-black and light mode lost its accent. v2 adds --accent-text (#4D6B00, 6.1:1 on white) for text and strokes, and inverts accent surfaces to ink carrying volt — the trick the v1 REST card already used.
  • Cards read as cards. --bg goes #F3F5F8 → #EDF0F5, the card border from 4.5% to 9%, and light gains the lit top edge (--card-lift) that dark already had.
  • One volt moment per screen. Volt is now reserved for the single primary action. Streaks, tags and active states use the tonal --accent-soft plus --accent-text.
  • Day one is designed. Once demo data stops shipping, the empty app is the first impression: a 1-of-3 hero, dashed empty boxes that say what will appear, and stat tiles that hold their shape.
  • The weight chart carries its weight. Gradient area, 3px trend, faded daily scatter, an annotated goal reference with ETA, a dashed projection and a haloed today marker.
  • Head-to-head diverges from a centre line. Seven paired bars became one axis: left is Thanos, right is you, bar length is the margin. Ties read as empty.
  • Grain and hero glow. A 3.5–5% noise overlay and a radial accent glow behind each hero.
